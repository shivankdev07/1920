✨ Mere Dil Ki Ek Baat

Tumhara mann jo chahe, wo karo. Padhai karo, Singing karo, Dancing karo, Sports khelo, ghumo, tahlo ya zindagi ko apne tareeke se jiyo.

Sahi ho ya galat, achha ho ya bura—main hamesha tumhare saath khada rahunga. Zindagi mein chahe kuch bhi ho jaye, chahe kitni bhi mushkilein kyun na aayein, main tumhara saath kabhi nahi chhodunga.

Tumhe jab bhi meri zarurat hogi, main hamesha tumhare saath khada milunga. Tumhare har faisle mein, har khushi mein, har pareshani mein aur har mushkil waqt mein.

Tum jo banna chaho bano, jo karna chaho karo aur jis raaste par chalna chaho chalo. Main tumhe kabhi rokunga nahi, kyunki main chahta hoon ki tum apni zindagi apne mann se jiyo aur wo sab karo jo tumhe khushi deta hai.

Aur ek baat hamesha yaad rakhna—main kahin bhi rahun ya tum kahin bhi raho, jab bhi tumhe meri zarurat padegi, tum mujhe apne paas hi paogi.

Chahe waqt kaisa bhi ho, haalat kaise bhi hon, main tumhare saath khada rehne ke liye hamesha taiyaar rahunga. Tumhari khushi mein khush, tumhare dukh mein tumhare saath aur har mushkil mein tumhara sahaara bankar.

Bas itna yaad rakhna ki tum kabhi akeli nahi ho. Main hamesha tumhare saath rahunga—tumhari parchhai bankar. ❤️
